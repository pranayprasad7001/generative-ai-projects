FACTUAL CHRONOLOGY: MILESTONES IN COMPUTING HISTORY
=====================================================

1945 - ENIAC (Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer) is completed at the University of Pennsylvania.
1957 - FORTRAN, the first high-level programming language, is released by IBM.
1969 - ARPANET, the precursor to the modern Internet, establishes its first successful host-to-host link.
1972 - Dennis Ritchie develops the C programming language at Bell Labs.
1983 - Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol (TCP/IP) becomes the standard network protocol for ARPANET.
1991 - Tim Berners-Lee introduces the World Wide Web project to the public.
2008 - The Satoshi Nakamoto whitepaper titled "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System" is published.
